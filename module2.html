01 · LEARN

What is a CMS?

CMS stands for Content Management System.

A CMS allows users to create, edit, organize and manage website content without having to build every part of the website from scratch.

🧩

Simple Example

Imagine a business owner wants to update the text on their About page.

With a CMS, they can usually log into an admin area, edit the content and publish the change without directly editing the website's source code.

What can a CMS help manage?

Pages

About, Services, Contact and other website pages.

Posts

Blog articles and other regular content.

Media

Images, videos and other media files.

Users

Admins, editors and other user roles.

Settings

Website configuration and preferences.

Extensions

Plugins, apps or other additional features.

03 · PLATFORM

Shopify

Shopify is a hosted e-commerce platform designed to help businesses create and manage online stores.

S
HOSTED E-COMMERCE PLATFORM

Shopify

Shopify provides store management features such as products, inventory, orders, payments and store customization through its platform.

💡 Important

Shopify is different from WordPress because Shopify is a hosted platform. The hosting infrastructure is part of the service, while WordPress itself is software that can be installed on suitable hosting.

05 · APPROACH

What is Custom Development?

Custom development means building a website or web application specifically according to a project's requirements rather than relying entirely on a pre-built CMS structure.

🛠️

When custom development may be considered

A project may need custom development when it has specialized workflows, integrations, functionality or technical requirements that are not easily handled by an existing platform.

Common characteristics

Custom Features

Functionality can be designed around specific requirements.

Custom UI

Interface and user experience can be built specifically for the project.

Integrations

Specialized systems and APIs can be integrated.

Development Effort

Usually requires more technical planning and development work.

COMPARE

CMS, E-Commerce Platform or Custom?

There is no single solution for every client. The correct approach depends on the project's requirements.

Option
Typical Use
Management
Considerations
WordPress Business websites, blogs, content websites Dashboard-based management Hosting, themes and plugins need management
Shopify Online stores Hosted store management Platform-based features and ecosystem
WooCommerce WordPress-based online stores WordPress + WooCommerce dashboard More control, but more components to manage
Custom Development Specialized websites and web applications Custom admin/development workflow Greater development effort and responsibility
CLIENT REQUIREMENT

Ask Before You Choose

01

What does the client need?

Business website, blog, online store or custom application?

02

Who will manage it?

Will the business owner or a marketing team update the website?

03

What features are required?

Products, bookings, memberships, integrations or other workflows?

04

What are the constraints?

Consider budget, timeline, maintenance and technical requirements.

🎯 Key idea

Don't choose a platform first and then force the client's requirements into it. Understand the requirements first, then select an appropriate approach.
