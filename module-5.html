AI Content Creation

Learn how to use AI as a practical content assistant for websites, e-commerce products, blogs, landing pages and content planning — while keeping human review and fact-checking at every stage.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

What You Will Learn

By the end of this module, students should be able to use AI to create and improve different types of digital content.

AI Copywriting

Use AI to create website copy, product descriptions, landing page content and blog drafts.

Prompt Writing

Create clear prompts using role, task, audience, context, tone, constraints and output format.

Content Planning

Generate useful blog ideas and a simple 7-day content calendar for a business.

Review & Fact-Check

Check AI output for accuracy, relevance, tone and unsupported claims before publishing.

CONCEPT

Where Can AI Help?

AI can support many parts of a digital marketer's content workflow. The final content should still be reviewed and edited by a human.

Website Content

Home page headlines, About sections, service descriptions, FAQs and calls to action.

Product Descriptions

Create structured product copy using real product details, audience and desired tone.

Blog Content

Generate topic ideas, outlines and first drafts that can later be edited and verified.

Landing Pages

Develop headline, supporting copy, benefits and CTA ideas for a campaign or product page.

Key idea: AI is a content assistant. It can help with speed and ideas, but students are responsible for checking the final content before it is published.
PROMPT WRITING

How to Write a Useful Prompt

A good prompt gives AI enough information to understand exactly what the marketer needs.

Role

Tell AI what role or perspective it should use.

Task

Clearly explain what you want AI to create.

Audience

Describe who will read or use the content.

Context

Give relevant business, product or campaign information.

Tone

Specify the writing style such as natural or professional.

Constraints

Set limits such as word count or claims to avoid.

Output Format

Specify headings, bullets, table, paragraph or another format.

Prompt Formula Role + Task + Audience + Context + Tone + Constraints + Output Format
LIVE DEMO

Demo: Women's Cotton Kurti

Use a real product example and show students how detailed instructions can produce more useful output.

SAMPLE AI PROMPT

Role: Act as an e-commerce copywriter.

Task: Write a product description for a women's cotton kurti.

Audience: Women in Kerala aged 20–35.

Context: The product is a comfortable cotton kurti suitable for everyday use. Use only the product information provided.

Tone: Natural, simple and conversion-focused.

Constraints: Do not make unsupported claims. Do not invent product features, certifications, discounts or specifications.

Output: Product title, short description, key features and CTA.

Teaching point: Students should give AI real information about the product instead of asking AI to invent missing facts.
PRACTICAL WORKFLOW

Generate → Review → Edit → Verify → Publish

Follow this workflow whenever AI is used for content creation.

01
Understand Know the business, product and content goal.
02
Prompt Give AI clear instructions and context.
03
Generate Create the first content draft.
04
Review Check facts, relevance and tone.
05
Publish Edit and use the final approved version.
QUALITY CHECK

Review AI Output Before Publishing

Students should never treat an AI response as automatically correct.

Check Facts

Verify prices, specifications, business details, dates and other factual information.

Check Audience

Make sure the language matches the target customer's needs.

Check Tone

Remove repetitive, unnatural or overly generic wording.

Edit Before Publishing

Add real business knowledge and make the content fit the actual website or product.

STUDENT PRACTICAL

Use AI on Your Own Project

Continue using the same business/project from Modules 1–4. The goal is to apply AI content creation to the real project.

01

Create 5 Product Descriptions

Select five products from your e-commerce project and create AI-assisted descriptions.

  • Use real product information.
  • Define the target audience.
  • Specify tone and output format.
  • Review and edit every result.
Deliverable: 5 reviewed product descriptions.
02

Create 2 Blog Ideas

Generate two useful blog topics related to your chosen business.

  • Blog title/topic.
  • Target audience.
  • Search intent.
  • Short content outline.
Deliverable: 2 blog ideas with outlines.
03

Create 1 Landing Page Draft

Create a landing page draft for one product, service or campaign.

  • Headline.
  • Supporting copy.
  • Benefits.
  • Call to action.
Deliverable: 1 edited landing page draft.
04

Create a 7-Day Content Calendar

Use AI to plan one week of content for the chosen business.

  • Day/date.
  • Content topic.
  • Content format.
  • Short caption or content idea.
Deliverable: 7-day content calendar.
